Privacy Policy

Syncboard · Last updated: July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Syncboard handles information when you use the application.

Information used by Syncboard

Syncboard uses the board content, settings, and sync information you choose to create or connect in order to provide the app’s core features. This may include titles, notes, tasks, shared-board content, and technical identifiers needed to keep your devices or collaborators in sync.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate, maintain, and improve Syncboard’s synchronization and collaboration features; prevent abuse or security issues; and respond to support requests you initiate.

Sharing

Content you choose to share may be visible to the people or services you authorize. Syncboard does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support the app’s operation, only as needed to provide those services or comply with applicable law.

Data retention and security

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide Syncboard and meet legal or security obligations. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information, but no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can stop using Syncboard at any time and may remove content or disconnect shared access through the app where those options are available. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information.

Children’s privacy

Syncboard is not intended for children under the age required to consent to data processing in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact me@zbd.com.tr.
