Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Race To End handles data on Wear OS and Apple Watch devices. The app is designed to keep information on your device and avoid unnecessary data collection.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Race To End does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, location, or identifiers.

2. No Tracking or Analytics

We do not use analytics SDKs, ad trackers, fingerprinting, or other monitoring tools. The app does not send usage data to our servers.

3. Local Storage Only

If the app saves preferences, scores, or app state, that information stays locally on your device using the platform storage provided by Wear OS or Apple Watch.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not depend on third-party services that collect user data. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before any such service is introduced.

5. Permissions

Race To End only requests permissions that are necessary for core app functionality. No permission is used for advertising or personal profiling.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: me@zbd.com.tr
